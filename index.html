WHAT THE JOIN TELLS YOU

Instruments, not conclusions.

Put chat, pull requests and product numbers on one clock and the picture stops being simple. Ask how much more got done and you get several answers — every one of them a defensible measurement. Most tools pick one. This one shows you all of them and lets you do the reading.

◎

One clock

Every series at the resolution its data actually has, side by side. A lane shown on its own asserts a conclusion by leaving the others out.

◈

Months, not before and after

Split a period in two and you can report a rise that the months show as a dip, then a recovery. Resolution is not a detail. It is the finding.

△

Missing is not zero

“We never measured this” and “the thing being measured stopped” look identical on most charts. They are different facts, so they are drawn differently.
